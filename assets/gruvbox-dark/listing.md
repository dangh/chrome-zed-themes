# Gruvbox Dark (Zed)

## Title (18/75)

Gruvbox Dark (Zed)

## Summary (111/132)

Chrome in Zed's Gruvbox Dark: title bar, tabs, toolbar and new tab page taken from the editor's own theme file.

## Description

Gruvbox Dark, lifted straight from the Zed editor's theme file, so Chrome and Zed can
sit side by side without a colour clash.

What's matched to the editor:

• Title bar and tab strip take Zed's title bar colour, with a soft gradient
  along the window's top edge
• Toolbar and the active tab take Zed's sidebar colour
• Inactive tabs carry no fill of their own — they disappear into the title
  bar, the way Zed's own tab bar reads, in both horizontal and vertical tab
  layouts
• The new tab page uses Zed's blank-window background
• The title bar holds its tone when the window loses focus, rather than
  washing out to a pale grey

Generated from Zed's published theme JSON rather than eyeballed, and checked
against real Chrome renders pixel by pixel.

Source and build script: https://github.com/dangh/chrome-zed-themes
